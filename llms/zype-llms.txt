# Zype: AI-enabled, API-first Video CMS for Mission-Critical Streaming


> Zype is a video content management system (CMS) and streaming platform used by enterprises and media leaders to power OTT apps, FAST channels, and global video distribution. With AI-enabled automation, developer-first APIs, and enterprise-grade reliability, Zype supports mission-critical streaming at scale.


Key terms:  
**Core Platform** → Video CMS, Video Streaming Platform, Multi-CDN Delivery  
**Monetization** → SSAI, DRM, Video Monetization, Advertising, Video CRM  
**Distribution** → FAST Channels, OTT Apps, Playout Automation, Linear Channels, App Builder  
**AI & Automation** → AI Subtitles, AI Translation, Workflow Automation, Video Analytics  
**Competitive Positioning** → Brightcove alternative, Vimeo alternative, JW Player alternative, Enterprise Streaming

## Zype Products


### Streaming Platform
- [Streaming Platform](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-streaming-platform): End-to-end video CMS for managing, delivering, and scaling streaming content.  
- [Encoding](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-streaming-platform/encoding): Cloud encoding tools to ensure high-quality playback on any device.  
- [Video CMS](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-streaming-platform/video-cms): Manage metadata for video organization, discovery, and workflows.  
- [Video CRM](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-streaming-platform/video-crm): Centralize viewer data to personalize and optimize engagement.  
- [Content Delivery](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-streaming-platform/video-content-delivery): Multi-CDN delivery for fast, reliable, and global streaming.  
- [Analytics](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-streaming-platform/analytics): Real-time insights into video performance and audience behavior.  


### Zype Playout
- [Zype Playout](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-playout): Cloud-based playout for building FAST and linear channels.  
- [Advanced API Tools](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-playout/advanced-api-tools): Developer APIs to automate playout workflows.  
- [Advertising](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-playout/advertising): Monetize channels with SSAI and dynamic ad insertion.  
- [Destinations](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-playout/playout-destinations): Deliver channels to OTT apps, FAST platforms, and O&O endpoints.  
- [Graphics](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-playout/graphics): Add overlays, branding, and visuals to streaming content.  
- [Channel Programming](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-playout/channel-programming): Schedule and automate programming for broadcast-style channels.  
- [Live Video Inserts](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-playout/live-video-insert): Insert live feeds into pre-programmed content.  


### Zype Apps Creator
- [Apps Creator](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-apps-creator): Build branded OTT apps without heavy engineering.  
- [TV Apps](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-apps-creator/tv-apps): Launch streaming apps on Smart TVs and connected TV devices.  
- [OTT Mobile Apps](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-apps-creator/ott-mobile-apps): Deliver iOS and Android apps for mobile audiences.  
- [OTT Web Apps](https://www.zype.com/product/zype-apps-creator/ott-web-apps): Stream content directly through web applications.
