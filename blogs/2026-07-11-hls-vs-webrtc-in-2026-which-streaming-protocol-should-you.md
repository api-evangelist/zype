One of the most significant advances of the streaming era has been the ability to move video across the internet reliably, at scale, and with increasingly low latency. Two protocols dominate that conversation: HTTP Live Streaming (HLS) and Web Real-Time Communication (WebRTC). They aren’t really competitors as much as tools built for opposite ends of the same problem.
