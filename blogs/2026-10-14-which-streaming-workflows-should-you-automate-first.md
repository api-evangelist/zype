Streaming operations teams have more automation options than ever: live playout, metadata tagging, distribution, live-to-VOD conversion, and more are all automatable to some degree. Deciding what to tackle first is harder than it looks, and the workflow that feels most urgent in a planning meeting isn't always the one consuming the most staff time. A short, structured look at where operational hours actually go tends to separate a high-value automation investment from one that saves less than expected.
