If you work in streaming media operations, content supply chains, or OTT platform management, you already know the reality: your day is spent bouncing between platforms. One minute you’re validating video metadata in Zype. The next you’re reviewing proxies in Iconik. Then you’re back in Zype building playlists, checking categories, or validating distribution feeds. Tabs multiply. Bookmarks pile up. Context switching slows you down. That exact friction is what inspired the creation of the Zype + Iconik Quick Links Chrome Extension — a lightweight productivity tool purpose-built to streamline navigation between two of the most critical systems in modern media workflows.
