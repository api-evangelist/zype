As broadcasters distribute local programming across websites, mobile apps, connected TV platforms, and other digital destinations, controlling where viewers can access that content becomes increasingly complex. A program may be available in one local market but restricted in another. Regional sports coverage can carry specific geographic requirements, while syndicated programming may be licensed only for selected areas.
