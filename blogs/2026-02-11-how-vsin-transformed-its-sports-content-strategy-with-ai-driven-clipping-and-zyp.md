Sports audiences don’t wait. They want the moment right now — the goal, the buzzer beater, the record-breaking swing — and they expect it everywhere they consume content. VSiN understood this reality. Their viewers were hungry for timely, context-rich insights, and their editorial teams understood the value of delivering more clips, faster, and across more platforms than ever before. The challenge was clear: How do you scale real-time sports clipping and distribution? To find the answer, VSiN began evaluating a series of next-generation clipping solutions. Each vendor brought promising capabilities, but VSiN needed more than basic clip creation. They required a full ecosystem approach — a workflow that was fast, automated, intelligent, and deeply integrated with their Zype streaming infrastructure.
