WordPress gives publishers a flexible foundation for building websites, managing editorial content, and reaching audiences online. But delivering a complete streaming destination requires more than placing a basic video player on a page. Media teams also need practical ways to organize and publish large video libraries, manage playlists, support live and linear programming, authenticate viewers, deliver entitled content, monetize premium programming, expose structured metadata, and maintain control over the presentation of every viewing experience.
