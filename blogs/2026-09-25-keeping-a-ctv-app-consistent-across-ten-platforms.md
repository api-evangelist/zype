Viewers expect a streaming service to work the same way everywhere. They expect to find the same content, recognize the navigation, and have access to the same features whether they open the app on Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, Samsung, LG, or Android TV. Delivering that consistency becomes harder after launch.
